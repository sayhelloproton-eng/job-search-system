# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and publication of another person's private employment or recruiting information are not acceptable.

When discussing bugs or examples, use synthetic data unless the owner of the data has explicitly made it public and redistribution is permitted.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that expose personal data, credentials, proprietary material or violate these expectations.
