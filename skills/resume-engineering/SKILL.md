# Resume Engineering Skill

## Truth boundary

A resume is a projection of approved Career Facts. A JD may choose and reorder facts; it may not create them.

```text
JD requirements
→ evidence selection
→ relevance ordering
→ concise quantified wording where supported
→ claim-boundary review
```

## Rules

- Never invent employers, dates, degree, scope, metrics, users, SLA, ownership or production status.
- Learning or a demo does not become historical production experience.
- Team outcomes and personal actions must be distinguishable.
- Keep qualifiers when they are material to truth.
- Prefer business / engineering value over tool-name dumping.
- Use STAR/XYZ/SMART as editing lenses, not as permission to manufacture numbers.
- If evidence is missing, mark the gap instead of filling it.

## Review

Before returning a targeted resume, check:

1. Every strong claim has evidence.
2. The top third matches the target JD.
3. Technical keywords are backed by real work.
4. Personal contribution is clear.
5. No unsupported percentage, scale or title is introduced.
