# Resume review rubric

Critical failures override scoring. Any invented fact, ownership inflation, unsafe metric, or confidential/internal leakage fails the candidate regardless of polish.

## Model reviewer — 100 points

### 1. Truth and evidence — 20
Every substantive claim is defensible from current Career Facts/Evidence and uses the correct confidence/scope.

### 2. JD relevance — 15
The resume proves the target's highest-value requirements instead of merely repeating keywords.

### 3. Technical signal — 15
Bullets reveal mechanisms, architecture, trade-offs, system boundaries, problem solving, or quality practices where relevant.

### 4. Ownership and seniority — 10
Personal responsibility is clear without title or management inflation; senior IC judgment is visible.

### 5. Recruiter first-screen clarity — 10
Target role, seniority, strongest domains, and proof points are obvious within the first screen/10–20 seconds.

### 6. Information density — 10
High-value facts dominate; weak, old, duplicated, or generic material is compressed or removed.

### 7. Human voice — 10
Natural Chinese, varied sentence structure, no noun piles, generic AI phrasing, exaggerated marketing language, or repetitive template syntax.

### 8. Structure and scanability — 5
Standard headings, clear chronology, natural terminology coverage, and no dependence on decorative presentation to make the content understandable.

### 9. Interview defensibility — 5
The candidate could explain every flagship bullet in ~30 seconds and expand it into a detailed project story.

## Candidate comparison

Do not collapse all candidates toward the highest model score. In an experiment, report score + weaknesses while preserving each candidate's hypothesis so the human can disagree.

## Human scoring sheet — 100 points

The user's score is the primary selection signal:

- 岗位匹配第一印象 — 25
- 技术含金量 — 20
- 真实可信 / 不像 AI 写的 — 20
- 信息密度与可读性 — 15
- 项目与经历吸引力 — 10
- 面试时自己能否自然讲出来 — 10

Also record:

- `最喜欢的一点`
- `最垃圾的一点`
- `是否愿意真的拿它投递：是/否`
- free-form comments

A low-scoring candidate is useful evidence about a failed narrative strategy; preserve the reason rather than merely deleting the file.
