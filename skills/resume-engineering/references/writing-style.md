# Technical resume writing style

Write for a technical hiring manager and recruiter scanning quickly, not for a benchmark prompt.

## Bullet construction

Recover the full causal story internally, then compress to the smallest useful statement:

`owned problem/scope + decisive action/mechanism + defensible result/consequence`

Use two bullets when one sentence would become an unreadable architecture dump.

Good technical bullets usually reveal at least two of these:

- what was difficult or broken;
- what decision the candidate made;
- how the mechanism actually worked;
- what system boundary or trade-off mattered;
- what changed after the work;
- what verified scope/metric demonstrates maturity.

## Avoid AI-template prose

Avoid repetitive structures such as every bullet starting with `负责`、`通过`、`基于`、`主导` or every sentence following identical STAR grammar.

Treat these phrases as warning signs when they add no specific meaning:

- 深度参与 / 深度负责
- 全面赋能
- 构建完整闭环
- 显著提升 / 大幅提升
- 端到端打通
- 沉淀能力
- 技术驱动业务
- 从 0 到 1 (unless the evidence and scope really justify it)

Replace generic language with the actual engineering object, mechanism, boundary, or consequence.

## Technical depth

Do not make a technical resume a technology inventory. A stack name earns space when it explains:

- architecture or system boundary;
- compatibility/migration strategy;
- performance/reliability mechanism;
- runtime/tooling behavior;
- developer workflow or quality control;
- why a trade-off was chosen.

## Senior IC signal

Senior technical signal comes from judgment, ownership, system scope, trade-offs, repeatability, quality, and cross-system delivery—not from adding management verbs.

Do not convert collaboration or technical ownership into formal people management.

## Readability

Use standard, easily understood headings and a logical reading order. Mirror exact JD terminology only when it is an accurate label for verified experience. Do not keyword-stuff or repeat skills to hit a quota.

## Chinese writing

Write natural Chinese technical prose. Do not translate English resume clichés literally. Keep sentences compact, use concrete nouns and verbs, and make project/ownership boundaries understandable without internal company context.
