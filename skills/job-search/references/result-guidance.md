# Result presentation guidance

## Search results

Present enough information to make a decision without exposing internal credentials or overwhelming the user.

Recommended columns:

| Priority | Job | Company | Salary | Location | Hard gates | Fit | Confidence | Key evidence | Main gap | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|

Always state collection time and whether evidence is live/current, saved historical JD, partial excerpt, or list-only metadata.

Do not show `security_id`, Cookie fields, tokens, raw headers, internal browser IDs, or authentication/routing data.

## Resume review

Lead with:

1. overall score and any blocking truth defect;
2. strongest signals;
3. highest-value improvements;
4. unsupported/ambiguous claims;
5. JD coverage when a target role exists;
6. recommended next version or action.

Prefer specific edits over generic advice such as “add more metrics” when the fact library does not contain verified metrics.
## Job evaluation

Use a compact decision card:

```text
Recommendation: STRONG_APPLY / APPLY / SELECTIVE / SKIP
Fit: <0-100>  Confidence: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW
Hard gates: ...
Strong evidence: ...
Gaps: ...
Resume base: ...
Next action: ...
```

The score must never be described as an offer/interview probability.

## Application package

Before any browser write, show the exact target, resume version/path, greeting/intro text, unresolved form answers, and planned irreversible action. Make the approval boundary visually obvious.

After execution report exactly one state: `SUBMITTED_CONFIRMED`, `NOT_SUBMITTED`, or `UNKNOWN`, plus the visible evidence used for that classification.

## Outcome report

Keep operational history separate from career facts. Record dates, observed stages, feedback, and next action. Clearly mark analysis such as “resume positioning may be weak” as inference rather than external fact.
