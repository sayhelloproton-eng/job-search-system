# Interview preparation and outcome learning

## Truth and narrative sources

Interview preparation consumes two trust tiers:

1. **Primary truth**: `career-assets/职业事实.md`, `事实台账.md`, and `career-assets/证据/`. These authorize factual, quantified, scope and ownership claims.
2. **Derived narrative**: `career-assets/面试/面试故事库.md`, JD-specific prep, answer banks and prior debrief notes. These may improve structure/phrasing, but never become a new fact source.

A number, scale or ownership claim from a derived source must trace to CF/EV evidence. If it cannot be confirmed, remove the number or mark it `USER_CANNOT_CONFIRM`; repetition never upgrades it.

## Exact application inputs

Use the saved JD/version, exact submitted resume, greeting/cover note actually sent, verified evidence, and prior feedback for this application. Do not prepare from a newer resume without marking the difference.

## Prepare → practice → real interview → debrief

### Prepare

Produce role/company focus, likely evaluation dimensions, 5–10 JD-linked questions, Story Bank candidates, honest bridge answers for real gaps, project deep-dive follow-ups, candidate questions, and claims requiring careful wording.

### Practice

When mock interviewing, ask one question at a time. Assess relevance, evidence, structure, technical depth, ownership clarity and overclaim risk. Tighten the answer using primary truth plus a compatible Story Bank narrative.

### Real interview

Treat what was actually asked as new market/interview evidence, not Career Fact evidence. Preserve stage/date, question themes and explicit interviewer feedback if the user provides it.

### Debrief

After an interview, capture: actual questions; where answers were strong/weak; which story was used; follow-ups that exposed missing depth; interviewer feedback; unresolved gaps; next-round prediction; and concrete preparation changes.

Debrief may update Story Bank phrasing/tags, the interview question bank, and learning priorities. It may not invent or upgrade career facts.

## Story Bank rules

Prefer 5–10 reusable, evidence-backed stories over creating a fresh fictional STAR for every JD. Match by problem/competency tags (ownership, migration, reliability, ambiguity, platformization, AI engineering, quality, conflict/collaboration, failure/recovery).

Every story keeps `Fact/Evidence`, `Claim status`, and `Provenance`. `VERIFIED` claims can be interview-facing; `NARRATIVE_ONLY`, `DERIVED_UNVERIFIED`, and `USER_CANNOT_CONFIRM` cannot authorize quantified/public claims.

## Outcome tracking

Record observable outcomes without interpretation inflation: recruiter contact/no response, submitted/rejected/closed, stage/date, faithfully summarized feedback, offer details the user chooses to track, and user decision/reason.

Repeated outcomes may improve strategy: poor fit results can tighten search; high-fit/low-callback can trigger resume review; repeated interview gaps can reprioritize learning; repeated hard-requirement rejection can change target segmentation. None of these directly rewrites Career Facts.

## Follow-up messages

Draft only when requested, from submitted material or verified facts. Never auto-send.
