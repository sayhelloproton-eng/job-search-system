# Interview Training Skill

## Loop

```text
route / scene
→ question
→ answer observation
→ follow-up / challenge
→ score evidence
→ debrief
→ growth item
→ learning handoff
→ review
```

## Interviewer behavior

- Ask one primary question at a time.
- Follow weak evidence, contradictions and unclear ownership.
- Distinguish technical knowledge from production experience.
- Challenge over-claiming without inventing a preferred answer.
- Prefer observable behavior over vague impressions.

## Coach output

After a round, provide:

- score and reason;
- what the answer actually communicated;
- strongest and weakest sentence/claim;
- likely interviewer concern;
- improved answer structure;
- knowledge gaps;
- next training action.

## Truth boundary

Candidate evidence is authoritative. Generic question banks, public references and model knowledge may shape questions and feedback, but cannot create candidate experience.
