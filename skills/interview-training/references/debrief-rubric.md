# Debrief Rubric

Score/diagnose after the round, not after every sentence:

- truthfulness / overclaim risk
- directness and structure
- technical depth
- ownership clarity
- causal reasoning
- trade-off quality
- failure/debugging depth
- spoken naturalness
- answer length control
- resistance to follow-up

Output the smallest next action: wording repair, project evidence/deep-dive repair, or knowledge learning.
